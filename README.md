# Retail Sales Analytics — Excel & Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to uncover trends in sales, profit, transactions, customer demographics, and product category performance.

The analysis was conducted using **Microsoft Excel** and **Microsoft Power BI**, with the goal of transforming raw retail data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project answers six key business questions:

1. What is the overall sales, profit, transaction, and profit-margin performance of the retail business?
2. How do sales and profit change over time, and which periods perform best or worst?
3. Which product category generates the highest sales, quantity sold, and profit?
4. How do customer demographics, particularly gender and age, influence sales and transactions?
5. What is the average transaction value, and which categories contribute most to revenue and profit?
6. What actionable recommendations can be made from the analysis?

## 📈 Key Findings

### Overall Performance

- **Total Sales:** ₦911,720
- **Total Profit:** ₦721,957.30
- **Total Transactions:** 1,997
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 5,018

### Category Performance

The three categories analyzed were:

- Electronics
- Clothing
- Beauty

Electronics generated the highest total sales, while Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold.

### Customer Demographics

The analysis examined sales and transaction performance by gender and age group.

Female customers generated slightly higher sales than male customers, while the **46–55 age group** recorded the highest sales and transaction count.

### Time-Based Performance

Sales and profit were analyzed across periods to identify the strongest and weakest performing months.

December recorded particularly strong performance, while some earlier months recorded comparatively lower sales and profit.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business can:

- Maintain strong-performing categories while monitoring category-level profitability.
- Investigate the factors contributing to strong December performance.
- Develop targeted marketing strategies for high-performing customer age groups.
- Monitor weaker periods and introduce promotional strategies where appropriate.
- Use customer demographic insights to improve sales targeting.
- Continue using data-driven dashboards for regular business performance monitoring.
